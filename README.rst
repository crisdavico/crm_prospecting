Holds the context an AI prospecting agent reads from Odoo and the fields
it fills on the leads it finds.

- A **Prospecting Profile** model: for one product, the ideal customer
  profile and the buying signals, as plain text with one item per line.
- A **Prospecting** tab on the UTM campaign form with the brief the
  agent reads: profile, industry (the contacts' industry list), country,
  states, city, company size, target count and extra instructions.
- A **Prospecting** tab on the lead form with what the agent produces:
  fit score (0 to 100), evidence, CUIT (used to detect duplicates) and
  the agent run reference.

Usage
=====

Everything lives under *CRM > Prospecting*, visible to sales users.

The module ships no products or profiles. Create each product you
prospect for, with its internal reference as a stable slug, and its
prospecting profile in *CRM > Prospecting > Profiles*. Sales managers
edit the profiles; sales users can read them.

To brief the agent, open *CRM > Prospecting > Campaigns*, open a
campaign, go to the *Prospecting* tab, pick a profile, an industry (the
same list contacts use) and a country, and fill in the target. With a
country set, only that country's states can be chosen. Campaigns can be
searched by country and industry, and grouped by country. Leads the
agent creates are linked to the campaign and carry their fit score and
evidence in the *Prospecting* tab of the lead.
