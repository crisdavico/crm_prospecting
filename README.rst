Holds the context an AI prospecting agent reads from Odoo and the fields
it fills on the leads it finds.

- A **Prospecting Profile** model: for one product, a free-text
  description of what the profile is, plus the ideal customer profile
  and the buying signals, as plain text with one item per line.
- A **Prospecting** tab on the UTM campaign form with the brief the
  agent reads: profile, industry (the contacts' industry list), country,
  states, city, company size, target count and the campaign goal,
  which focuses the agent's lead search.
- An **Open for Prospecting** option on campaign stages: the agent only
  works on campaigns in stages where it is on.
- A **Prospecting** tab on the lead form with what the agent produces:
  fit score (0 to 100), CUIT and LinkedIn profile (both used to detect
  duplicates) and the agent run reference. The agent's evidence for a
  lead goes in the lead's standard Notes (the ``description`` field).
- **Duplicate detection** for the agent:
  ``crm.lead.prospecting_create_lead(vals)`` creates the lead unless its
  person already has one, and answers
  ``{"status": "created" | "duplicate", "lead_id": id}``. A lead is one
  person at one company: the same person is their own email (a shared
  inbox such as ``info@`` identifies nobody), their LinkedIn profile, or
  their name at the same company, matched by CUIT, website or company
  name, ignoring case and accents. Every lead and opportunity counts, in
  any campaign, including lost and archived ones. Another person at the
  same company is a new lead. A lead without a person is the company
  itself. The search reads past the caller's record rules but answers
  only the matching lead's id; the caller must be able to create leads,
  and creates with their own permissions.
- **Customer emails** for the agent:
  ``crm.lead.prospecting_send_email(lead_id, subject, body)`` sends one
  email to the lead's customer and logs it on the lead's chatter. It goes
  to the lead's contact when it has an email, otherwise to the lead's own
  email without creating a contact. The body is plain text: it is escaped
  and its line breaks kept. The email is authored by the caller, with
  their signature, and sent right after the request commits; internal
  followers get their usual notification, customer followers nothing.
  Lost, archived and email-less leads are refused. The same subject and
  body sent to the lead in the last 24 hours is not sent again unless it
  failed. It answers
  ``{"status": "queued" | "duplicate", "message_id": id, "email": address}``;
  ``crm.lead.prospecting_email_status(message_id)`` then answers
  ``"sent"`` once the SMTP server accepted it, ``"failed"`` with a
  ``failure_reason``, or ``"pending"``.

Usage
=====

Everything lives under *CRM > Prospecting*, visible to sales users.

The module ships no products or profiles. Create each product you
prospect for, with its internal reference as a stable slug, and its
prospecting profile in *CRM > Prospecting > Profiles*. Sales managers
edit the profiles; sales users can read them.

To brief the agent, open *CRM > Prospecting > Campaigns*, open a
campaign, go to the *Prospecting* tab, pick a profile, an industry (the
same list contacts use) and a country, and fill in the target. With a
country set, only that country's states can be chosen. Campaigns can be
searched by country and industry, and grouped by country.

The agent only works on campaigns whose stage is open for prospecting.
*CRM > Prospecting > Stages* lists the campaign stages with their *Open
for Prospecting* option; stages start closed. Sales users can see the
stages; changing them takes the Administration > Settings access that
Odoo requires for campaign stages. Leads the agent creates are linked to the campaign and carry their fit score in
the *Prospecting* tab of the lead; the agent's evidence goes in the
lead's standard *Notes* tab (the ``description`` field).
